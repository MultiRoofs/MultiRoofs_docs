# General Overview

## 1. Purpose and target audience

The [Strategic Priorities Tool](https://stedelijke-opgaven.pages.dev/) is a web-based tool intended exclusively for local authorities. Its purpose is to map urban challenges within local authorities and ultimately help determine which rooftop functions should be implemented and where.

Within the MultiRoofs project, the tool supports the assessment of urban priorities as a basis for future rooftop planning decisions. Building owners are not the intended users of the tool.

### Score areas

The Score areas section allows users to assign priority scores to urban challenges for different urban area types. The challenges are displayed in rows, and the area types are displayed in columns.

Examples of area types include Historical City Centre, Modern City Centre, Village Centre, Urban residential, and Rural areas.

The scoring scale is:

| Score | Priority |
|---|---|
| 1 | Very low |
| 2 | Low |
| 3 | Medium |
| 4 | High |
| 5 | Very high |
| 0 or empty | Not applicable; excluded from averages |

The interface uses colors to distinguish scores and displays an average score for each main challenge category within each area type.

### Urban challenge categories

- Climate Adaptation and Resilience
- Loss of Nature and Biodiversity
- Decarbonizing Energy Systems
- Aging, Unfit, or Fragile Infrastructure
- Sustainable and Equitable Mobility
- Affordable Housing
- Transition to a Circular Economy
- Urban Health and Well-being
- Social Inequality and Segregation
- Governance
