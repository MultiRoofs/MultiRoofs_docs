# Owners and Incentives

A roof may be needed by the city and technically suitable, yet nothing happens unless its owner decides to act. In the MultiRoofs methodology, this is the **"Want"**: the interests, constraints and decision logic of building owners, users and institutions, and the measures public authorities can use to influence them.

City priorities and owner priorities do not always match. A municipality may see heat reduction or biodiversity as essential in a district, while owners there care more about energy savings, property value or low maintenance. A well-designed rooftop proposal can fail if it does not appeal to the people who must approve and pay for it. Understanding owners, and designing incentives that bridge this gap, is therefore as important as mapping needs and capacities.

## Owners and incentives in the roadmap

The assessment of the owners and available incentives is considered in steps 6 and 8 of the [8-step rooftop activation roadmap](../about-multiroofs/index.md).

**Step 6. Match opportunities with challenges.** Examine where the opportunities recorded in the rooftop passports, grouped by building type and owner type, overlap with the urban challenges at district or city level. This produces scenarios that show which groups of roofs and owners can contribute to which priorities.

**Step 8. Design targeted incentives.** List the instruments that could encourage owners to activate their roofs (financial, advisory, regulatory and communication measures), and direct them at the districts, building types and owner types with the highest priority.
