# Digital twins

A digital twin is a digital representation of a real object, system or place that is kept up to date with data from its real-world counterpart. Digital twins began in aerospace and manufacturing, and are now increasingly used by cities to understand their buildings, infrastructure and environment, and to test decisions before making them.

In MultiRoofs, the digital twin brings together 3D building models, geographic data, local priorities and stakeholder input in one visual model. It helps public authorities see the potential of rooftops, compare scenarios and discuss options with colleagues and owners. It is an **enabling instrument, not a decision-maker**: the choices remain with the public authority.

This section explains the concepts behind digital twins, so that you can understand what the MultiRoofs tools do, what they can and cannot tell you, and what it takes to build and maintain a digital twin in your own organisation.
