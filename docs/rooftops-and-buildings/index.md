# Rooftops and Buildings

Once a city knows what it needs, the next question is what its rooftops can offer. In the MultiRoofs methodology, this is the **"Can"**: the physical, technical and regulatory capacity of specific roofs, buildings and districts.

Three elements describe this capacity:

- **Rooftop functions:** what a roof can be used for, classified with a seven-colour system.
- **Building characteristics:** the features of a building that make certain functions feasible or not, such as its use, age, roof type and structure.
- **Urban districts:** the type of area where the building is located, which shapes both local needs and the building stock.

## Assessing capacity: roadmap steps 4 and 5

The assessment of the city's capabilities is considered in steps 4 and 5 of the [8-step rooftop activation roadmap](../about-multiroofs/index.md).

**Step 4. Map owners and buildings.** Classify the building stock by building type and owner type, using the MultiRoofs typologies. To limit the effort, this can be done only for the districts that ranked as most urgent when identifying needs.

**Step 5. Build a rooftop passport.** For each roof, gather all available city and district data into a rooftop passport. The passport summarises the spatial, technical, organisational and financial conditions of the roof.

The [MultiRoofs Builder](../tools/CityJSON/index.md) supports these steps by generating 3D models of buildings and their roofs from LiDAR data and building footprints.
